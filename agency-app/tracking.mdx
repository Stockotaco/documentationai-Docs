## Overview

The **Tracking setup** screen walks through getting one client's tracking
live. Open a client and choose **Tracking**. Each step checks itself and
shows whether it is done.

## Install the snippet

Copy the snippet and paste it into the `<head>` of every page on the client's
site, or into the site builder's header code setting. The snippet loads Mythic
with the client's publishable key, which is safe on a public website.

## Verify events are arriving

This step shows the time of the last event and the number of events in the
last 24 hours. It turns done once the first event arrives, usually within a
minute of someone loading a page. Click **Check again** to refresh.

If nothing arrives, check that the snippet is in the page's `<head>` on the
live site, not only in a draft. **Ask agent to diagnose** opens the chat panel
to work through it with you.

## Serve from your own domain

Recommended. Tracking runs from a subdomain of the client's site, such as
`data.acmeplumbing.com`, instead of Mythic's domain. Browsers and ad blockers
treat it as part of the client's site, so fewer events are lost.

1. Enter the **Tracking hostname** and click **Add hostname**.
2. Add the CNAME record shown under **Point DNS at Mythic** at the client's
   DNS provider.
3. Mythic issues the certificate once the record resolves. **DNS**,
   **Certificate** and **Verified** turn done when it is ready.

**Remove** stops serving from the hostname.

## Connect GoHighLevel

For clients on GoHighLevel. Click **Connect GoHighLevel** and choose the
client's sub-account. Mythic then receives CRM events such as new contacts
and opportunity changes, and ties them to the same people as the website
events. **Reconnect** fixes a connection that shows **Disconnected**.

## Privacy and capture

- **Add IP and location at the edge**: adds the visitor's IP address and
  location to every event, including the first one of a visit. Meta uses
  these to match conversions. It does nothing for HIPAA clients.
- **Autocapture clicks and forms**: records clicks and form submissions
  without extra code. This setting overrides the site's own snippet settings.
- **Record session replays**: records visits for the
  [Sessions](/agency-app/sessions) screen. Recordings are kept 30 days, typed
  values are masked, and visits under 10 seconds are not recorded. **Visits
  recorded** sets the share of visits to record, from every visit down to
  10%.
- **Record heatmaps**: records clicks and scroll depth for the
  [Heatmaps](/agency-app/heatmaps) screen. It is off by default because it
  adds data for every click and scroll.
- **Allowed sites**: the hostnames that may send events for this client,
  comma separated. Subdomains are included. Leave it empty to accept any site.
