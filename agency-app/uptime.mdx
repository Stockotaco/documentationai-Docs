## Overview

Mythic checks the destination of every short link every 5 minutes. The
**Link uptime** screen shows the result. There is nothing to turn on: a link
is monitored as soon as it is created, as long as it is enabled and not
expired.

Open **Link uptime** from the agency menu to see every client's links, or from a
client's menu to see only that client's.

## Read the screen

The tiles at the top show:

- **Monitored links**: links checked in the last 24 hours.
- **Down now**: links whose latest check failed.
- **Uptime**: the share of checks that succeeded, over the last 30 days or
  since the link was created, whichever is shorter.

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Link** | The link's title or short path, and the destination URL it points to |
| **Status** | **Up**, or **Down** with the HTTP status code or the error |
| **Uptime 24h** | The share of checks that succeeded in the last 24 hours |
| **Response** | How long the destination took to answer the latest check |
| **Checked** | When the latest check ran |

A client's screen also charts the daily average response time for the last 14
days.

## When a link goes down

A link counts as down after **2 failed checks in a row**, so one slow answer
doesn't raise an alert. Mythic then opens a critical alert, such as "Short
link /spring-sale is down", and sends it to your alert channels. When the
destination answers again, the alert resolves. A rule can also send that
recovery.

Choose where alerts go, a Slack channel or a webhook, on the **Alerts**
screen.

## Common causes

- **404**: the page the link points to was moved or deleted. Update the
  link's destination, or restore the page.
- **5xx or no response**: the client's site is down or blocking the check.
  Some firewalls block automated requests. Ask the client's host to allow
  them.

## For developers

Uptime is available on the [Client Links API](/client-links-api/overview),
with `link_down` and `link_up` webhooks. Alert routing is on the
[Alerts API](/agency-api/alerts).
