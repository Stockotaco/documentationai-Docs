## Overview

The **Heatmaps** screen shows where visitors click and how far they scroll on
a client's site, over the last 30 days. Open a client and choose
**Heatmaps**.

Heatmap capture is off by default and is turned on per client with **Record
heatmaps** in [Tracking setup](/agency-app/tracking). If it is off, the
screen says so and links there. Pages appear once capture is on and visitors
click or scroll.

## Read a heatmap

1. Choose a **Page**. Each page shows its clicks and sessions.
2. Choose a **Mode**:
   - **Clicks**: where visitors clicked. Hotter colors mean more clicks.
   - **Scroll depth**: how far down the page visitors got.
3. Narrow it down with **Layout** (Mobile, Tablet or Desktop) and **Source**.
   Both list only the values that reached this page, with their session
   counts.

**Sessions that landed here** opens [Sessions](/agency-app/sessions) filtered
to visits that started on the chosen page.

The heat is drawn on a recorded visit of the page, so it lines up with the
real layout. The note under the heatmap says which visit was used. If there is
no recording for the chosen layout, the heat is drawn on a blank page of that
width.

Very long pages show their top part only, and a banner says how much is
shown.

## Clicked elements

Under the click map, a table groups clicks by the element that was clicked,
with **Clicks** and **Visitors** for each. Only clicks from tracking snippet
version 3.26 or later have element data. Older clicks have positions only.

## Empty heatmaps

- **No clicks for this filter**: try another layout or source.
- **No scroll measurements**: scroll depth is saved when a visitor leaves the
  page, so a page can have clicks before it has scroll data.

## For developers

See the [Heatmaps API](/heatmaps-api/overview). Turn capture on with
`PUT /client/v1/settings/heatmaps`.
