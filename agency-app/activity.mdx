## Overview

The **Activity** screen is a live feed of events, newest first, from the last
7 days. Use it to check that tracking works and to see what visitors are
doing right now.

- From the agency, **Activity** shows the newest 200 events across every
  client.
- Open a client and choose **Activity** to see the newest 100 events from
  that client.

## The feed

Each row shows the **Event**, the **Person** (or **Anonymous**) and the
**Time**. The agency feed adds the **Client**, and a single client's feed adds
the **Source**. The **URL** shows when the table has room. With the chat panel
open it is hidden, and it stays in the event's **Page & device** tab. Hover a
cut-off name to see it in full. With **Live** on, the feed refreshes every 30
seconds and new events slide in at the top. Turn **Live** off to stop it
while you read. **Reload** refreshes it now.

- **Event type**: show only one event, such as `form_submitted`.
- **Internal events**: off by default. It hides Mythic's own background
  events, such as session recording and page performance, and GoHighLevel tag
  changes. The label shows how many are hidden. Page views and identify
  events always show.

If a client has sent no events, check its [Tracking setup](/agency-app/tracking).

## An event

Click a row to see everything Mythic captured for that event:

- **Properties**: the data sent with the event.
- **Page & device**: URL, referrer, UTM source, medium and campaign, click
  id, device, location and the tracking library.
- **Person**: who sent it, whether they are identified, a link to the
  session, and their sessions and first visit.
- **Deliveries**: each destination the event was sent to, with its status and
  how long it took. Agency only.
- **Raw**: the event exactly as stored.

**Ask agent** asks the assistant in the chat panel about the event.
