## Overview

The **Correlation** screen compares a client's metrics over time, such as ad
spend and new-customer revenue. It shows how closely they move together,
whether that is holding lately, and how long one takes to show up in the
other. Open a client and choose **Correlation**.

Correlation reads the client's BigQuery dataset. If the client does not have
one yet, see [Exports](/agency-app/exports).

## Ask a question

Start from one of the suggested questions, such as **Does spend bring
visits?**, or set it up yourself:

- **Metrics**: pick 2 to 5. The first is the one that leads in the lead time
  scan.
- **Window**: the last 30, 90, 180 or 365 days.
- **Grain**: compare by **Day**, **Week** or **Month**.
- **Rolling window**: how many points each "lately" reading uses. Leave it
  empty for the default. 0 turns it off.
- **Max lag**: how far to shift one metric when looking for a lead time.
  Leave it empty for the default.
- **First-time lookback (days)**: for new-customer metrics, the history
  checked before the window so returning customers do not count as new.

## Read the result

With more than two metrics, choose the **Pair** to look at. For each pair:

- **Overall**: the correlation (r) from -1 to 1, with its strength and
  direction. Near 1 means the metrics rise together. Near -1 means one rises
  as the other falls. Near 0 means no clear link.
- **Lately**: the correlation over the most recent rolling window, and
  whether it is **Holding**, **Weakening** or **Strengthening**.
- **Lead time**: how long the first metric takes to show up in the second,
  or **None**.
- **Trend**: both metrics over the window.
- **Correlation over time**: the rolling correlation.
- A table of r at each shift, with the **Best** one marked.

**Ask agent** asks the assistant in the chat panel to explain the result.

## Warnings

| Warning | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Too few points** | There is not enough data for a reliable answer. Try a longer window or a finer grain. |
| **A series never changes** | One metric is flat, so it cannot correlate. |
| **A metric starts or stops mid-window** | One metric has data for only part of the window. |
| **No real lead time** | The best shift barely beats no shift. The metrics move together in the same period, with no delay. |
| **The peak may be further out** | The best shift is at the edge of the scan. Try a larger max lag. |
| **A few outliers drive the fit** | A few unusual points account for most of the correlation. |

Correlation shows that metrics move together. It does not prove that one
causes the other. To measure what ads really add, see
[Experiments](/agency-app/experiments).
