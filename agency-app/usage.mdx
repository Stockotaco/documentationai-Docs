## Overview

The **Usage & billing** screen shows your subscription, your plan, event
volume this month and the API requests made with your agency's keys. Choose
**Usage & billing** in the agency navigation.

## Billing

The billing card shows the state of your subscription:

- **Subscribe** starts a subscription.
- **Connect my subscription** links a subscription you already bought. Use
  the same email you sign in with.
- **Manage billing** opens the billing portal in a new tab to change your
  plan, card or invoices.

If a payment fails, the card asks you to update your card. If the
subscription ends, reports and data reads are paused until you renew.

## Plan and volume

| Tile | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Standard clients** | Clients on the Standard plan. |
| **Pro clients** | Clients on the Pro plan. |
| **Plan** | Your plan, with events used against the allowance where it has one. |
| **Events this month** | Events received this month across all clients. |

## API requests

**API requests per day** charts requests made with your agency's API and MCP
keys, by API. This counts requests, not events. The table under it lists
each route with its request count and **5xx** server errors.

Keys are managed on the [API & MCP keys](/agency-app/keys) screen.
