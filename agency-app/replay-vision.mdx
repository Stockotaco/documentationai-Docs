## Overview

A **scanner** is a question an AI model answers by watching a session
recording: did the visitor hit an error, what did they come to do, how engaged
were they. Each answer is a **result**. Results are also sent into the
client's data as a `$recording_observed` event, so you can count and chart
them like any other event.

Scans run on your own OpenRouter or Gemini key, and the provider bills you
directly.

Open **Replay Vision** from the agency menu to see every client's scanners, or
from a client's menu to see only that client's. The hub has six tabs:

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **What to watch** | The top recordings to watch from the latest answered results, ranked monitor yes first, then low scores, friction and second-check disagreements. Filter by date range, client, scanner, type and tag. |
| **Scanners** | Results per day, scans used against the monthly limits, reported spend, and a table of scanners with an **Automatic** switch, scans this month, spend and second-check disagreements. |
| **Usage** | Reported model spend this month, a straight-line estimate to month end, scans used and when the limit resets, per scanner and per Scout. |
| **Reports** | Every Scout report, newest first, with its priority. |
| **All results** | Every scanner's results, with status and second-check filters. |
| **Model keys** | Your OpenRouter or Gemini keys. |

<Callout kind="note">
These screens arrive with the Replay Vision Scouts release of the Mythic API.
If a tab shows an error, your agency's API is not on that release yet.
</Callout>

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a model key">
    Open the **Model keys** tab and add an OpenRouter or Gemini API key. A key
    added at the agency level is used by every client. A key added on a client
    is used for that client instead. Mythic checks the key with the provider,
    encrypts it, and never shows it again.

    A Gemini key must be on a paid plan. Google may use free-tier content to
    improve its products, and recordings show your clients' visitors.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a scanner">
    Click **New scanner**, choose the client and the type, and write the
    question. Describe what can be seen, not feelings. "Answer yes if the
    visitor clicked Submit and an error appeared" works. "Answer yes if the
    visitor was confused" does not. Say what to answer when the recording
    doesn't show enough.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it on a few recordings">
    Open the scanner, go to the **Run** tab, click **Scan recordings** and pick
    recordings you have already watched.
    Results appear within a few minutes. Read the reasoning, and edit the
    question until the answers match what you saw.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on automatic scanning">
    On the scanner's **Run** tab, switch on **Scan new recordings
    automatically** and set the sample, the
    share of new recordings to scan. Every 5 minutes the scanner picks from
    the recordings that just ended.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Scanner types

| Type | Answers with | Example question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Monitor** | Yes or no, or "inconclusive" if you allow it | Did the visitor hit an error? |
| **Classifier** | One or more of your tags | Why did they come: browsing, buying, account, unclear |
| **Scorer** | A number on your scale | How engaged were they, 0 to 10 |
| **Summarizer** | A title, summary, intent, outcome, friction points and keywords | What happened in this visit? |

Every answer includes the model's confidence, its reasoning, and the key
moment in the recording, with a link to the session.

## Review results

Click a scanner to open its page:

- **Overview**: the latest Scout report, status and last automatic scan, scans
  used of this scanner's monthly limit, its settings, and findings per day.
- **Observations**: this scanner's results. Click one to open it.
- **Run**: scan picked recordings and turn on automatic scanning.
- **Scouts**: the scanner's [Scouts](#scouts).
- **Alerts**: alert settings for a Monitor scanner.

A result's page shows the question, the answer with its confidence, reasoning
and cited moments under **Summary**, and the cited moments in time order under
**Timeline**. The recording plays beside it; clicking a cited moment seeks the
player there. Use **Previous result** and **Next result** to move through the
list you came from, and **Copy link** to share it.

A client's **Sessions** screen shows the results for each recording, and can
scan a single session.

### Rate results

Mark an answered result **Useful** (thumbs up) or **Not useful** (thumbs down).
Click again to clear it. Ratings are shared by everyone in your agency; the
last change wins. They help you judge a scanner's question. They do not retrain
the model.

### Second check

After your model answers, a second model reads the answer's reasoning and the
visit's timeline and checks whether they support the answer. Results where the
two disagree are marked **Check disagrees**. Watch those recordings first. A
scanner with many disagreements usually has a vague question.

Agreement does not prove the answer is right. The second check does not see
the video. It catches an answer that its own reasoning doesn't support, or
reasoning the timeline contradicts.

## Send findings to Slack or a webhook

For a **Monitor** scanner, open its **Alerts** tab, turn on **Raise an alert** and save. A yes answer
raises a warning alert unless the second check disagrees. Classifiers,
scorers and summarizers do not raise alerts.

Click **Set up Slack or webhook notifications** to open the guided setup with
**Replay Vision findings** selected. Connect a destination, test delivery and
choose the clients to receive notifications for. See [Alerts](/agency-app/alerts).

## Limits and costs

- Each scanner scans up to its monthly limit (500 by default), automatic and
  picked scans together. The scanner shows this month's scans, answers,
  failures and model spend.
- **Scan recordings** takes up to 50 recordings at a time. It ignores the
  sample and counts against the monthly limit.
- A scanner scans each recording once.
- Recordings shorter than 15 seconds, with less than 10 seconds of activity,
  or with more than an hour of activity are not eligible. They are not sent to
  your model and don't count against the limit.
- Recordings are kept 30 days. HIPAA clients have no recordings.
- A client can have up to 25 scanners.
- Editing a scanner's question, settings or model starts a new version.
  Earlier results keep the version they were scanned with.
- Deleting a scanner deletes its results and Scouts, but frozen Scout reports/evidence are retained. Events already sent stay in the client's data.

## Scouts

A **Scout** is a scheduled reviewer for one scanner. Scanners review sessions;
a Scout reviews the scanner's results. On its schedule it reads the scanner's
new results, plus its own last three reports, and files one report: a title,
a summary, findings ranked by how many sessions they cover, and a priority from
P0 to P4. Each finding links to the recording at the cited moment.

To add one, open a scanner's **Scouts** tab and click **Set up a scout**. Pick
a template, then fill in:

- **Instructions**: what to look for. Templates: Daily digest, Weekly themes,
  Root cause, Trend watch, New issue watch, or start from scratch (replace the
  placeholders in capitals).
- **Schedule**: every day, weekdays, or weekly, at a time in a timezone.
- **Delivery**: optional Slack or webhook channels that receive each report.

**Run now** is available only before the first report. You can pause, edit or
delete a Scout; deleting it keeps its reports. The latest report appears on the
scanner's **Overview**, and every report is under the hub's **Reports** tab.

Scouts read result text, not new recordings, using the scanner's model and
key. They cost extra on your key but do not use the monthly scan limit. Each
run reads up to 100 of the oldest new results; anything left over is read by
the next run, and the report says when it covered only part of the backlog.
Reports compare scanner versions separately.

"No new observations" means there was nothing to read, so no model call was
made. "Nothing notable" means the model read the results and found nothing
worth reporting. Each report is delivered to each channel at most once. Scout
reports do not raise monitor alerts.

## Usage and spend

The **Usage** tab shows dollars your provider reported, next to scan counts.
There is no dollar limit; the monthly limits count scans.

- The **Reported spend** card counts scans started this UTC month, the same
  basis as the scan limits.
- The cumulative chart and the month-end estimate use scans by the day they
  finished. A scan started on the last day of a month and finished the next
  day counts in the card for the first month and in the chart for the second.
- Some providers don't report a cost (direct Gemini doesn't). The tab says how
  many scans or Scout calls reported one, and never shows unknown cost as $0.
- Scout spend includes retries and failed attempts.
- Usage covers current scanners only. Deleting a scanner removes its scan and
  Scout spend from the totals. Your provider's billing is the record of what
  you paid.

Reports can keep visitor-derived evidence after a scanner is deleted. Deleting
a Scout is not personal-data erasure; contact Mythic for erasure.

## For developers

The same scanners, keys and results are available on the
[Replay Vision API](/agency-api/replay-vision) and as MCP tools.
Paged observation/report MCP lists return `{data,next_cursor}`; follow the
cursor until null. Rating responses use `observation_id`, `rating`, `rated_at`.
Report and evidence links open in the agency app; replay links put epoch
milliseconds in `?t=` and may outlive the 30-day recordings.
