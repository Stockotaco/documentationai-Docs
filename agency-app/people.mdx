## Overview

The **People & segments** screen lists the visitors Mythic has seen on a
client's site, identified and anonymous. Open a client and choose **People**.
The screen has three tabs: **People**, **Segments** and **Journeys**.

## People

Search by name, email or company. Each row shows:

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Person** | Name, email or user id. **Identified** means Mythic has an email or user id for them. **Anonymous** means it has neither yet. |
| **First source** | The channel of their first visit. |
| **Last source** | The channel of their most recent visit. |
| **Sessions** | How many visits they have made. |
| **Last seen** | When they last visited. |

When a visitor later fills in a form or is identified another way, Mythic
links their earlier anonymous visits to the same person.

## A person's profile

Click a person to open their profile:

- **Profile**: email, user id, first and last seen, sessions, devices and
  country.
- **Identities**: every identifier Mythic has linked to this person, with its
  type.
- **First touch** and **Last touch**: the channel, source and medium,
  campaign, referrer, landing page and device of their first and latest
  visits.
- **Events**: everything they did, newest first, with the page and source of
  each event.

## Segments

A segment is a saved set of person filters, such as everyone with an email who
came from paid search. Membership is worked out live, so a segment always
reflects the latest data. Each card shows the segment's size.

- **View members** opens the People tab filtered to the segment. Click
  **Show everyone** to clear it.
- **Ask agent** asks the assistant in the chat panel about the segment.
- **New segment** builds one from these filters. People must match every
  filter you set:
  - **Has an email**
  - **Last-touch utm_source**, for example `google`
  - **Minimum sessions**
  - **Active within (days)**

Deleting a segment removes its definition. The people in it are not affected.
For more complex segments, ask the assistant to build one.

## Journeys

Journeys show the paths people took before they converted.

1. Choose a **Goal event**, such as a purchase or a form submission. Goals
   need at least one conversion in the last 30 days.
2. For GoHighLevel appointment and opportunity status events, choose an
   **Outcome**, such as an appointment that showed or an opportunity that was
   won.
3. Optionally choose **Revenue from** to add revenue, such as the GoHighLevel
   order or payment amount.
4. Choose **Label journeys by** (Channel, Source / medium or Campaign) and a
   **Goal range** of 30 or 90 days.

The screen then shows:

- How many people reached the goal, how many of them have a tracked journey,
  the median time to convert, and revenue if chosen.
- **Paths to** the goal: the most common journeys, one step per visit, oldest
  first, with people, share, revenue and time to convert. Mythic looks back up
  to 90 days before each conversion. Visits referred by the client's own site
  do not count as touches.
- **People by** channel, source or campaign, credited by **First touch**,
  **Last touch** or **Split evenly** across every touch.
