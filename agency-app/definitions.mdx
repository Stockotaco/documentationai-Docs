## Overview

A **metric definition** says what a business term means for one client. For
example, a lead might be "a contact created from any form", and revenue might
be "the sum of `value` on `order_completed`". The assistant reads these
definitions before it builds any chart, so "how many leads last week?" gets
the same answer every time. Open a client and choose **Metric definitions**.

## Add a definition

1. Click **Add definition**.
2. Enter a **Name**, such as `Lead`, and an optional **Description**.
3. Pick the **Event**. The list shows this client's real events with their
   counts for the last 30 days.
4. Pick the **Measure**:
   - **Count of events**
   - **Unique users**
   - **Unique sessions**
   - **Sum of a property** or **Average of a property**, then pick the
     **Property**
5. Optionally click **Add filter** to count only some events. Pick a
   property, an operator (such as **is**, **is one of**, **contains** or **is
   set**) and a value. For **is one of**, separate values with commas.
6. Check the preview of the last 30 days, then click **Add definition**.

## Edit or delete

Each definition shows its name and a plain description of what it counts.
Click **Edit** to change it. Deleting a definition stops the assistant from
using it. Mythic data is not touched.
