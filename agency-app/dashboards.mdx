## Overview

A **dashboard** is a set of panels for one client. Each panel is a saved
insight from the client's [insight library](/agency-app/insights). Open a
client and choose **Dashboards** to see the list, then click one to open it.

## Build a dashboard

1. Click **New dashboard**. It opens as "Untitled dashboard".
2. Click **Add panel** and pick a saved insight.
3. Repeat for each panel.

**Edit with agent** opens the chat panel to change the dashboard by
describing what you want. The assistant can create the insights it needs and
add them as panels.

Each panel's menu has **Move earlier**, **Move later** and **Remove from
dashboard**. Removing a panel leaves the insight in the library.

The menu next to **Add panel** has **Duplicate** and **Delete**. Deleting a
dashboard keeps its insights in the insight library.

## Date range

Use **7d**, **30d** or **90d** to change the range of every panel at once.
Panels with a comparison show the change against the previous period.

## Share with the client

Turn on **Visible to client** to show the dashboard in the client's portal.
Shared dashboards show **Shared** in the list. Clients see only the
dashboards you share, and cannot edit them.

## For developers

Dashboards and insights are on the [Builder API](/builder-api/overview).
