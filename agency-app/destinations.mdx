## Overview

A **destination** sends a client's events from Mythic's servers to another
platform: the Meta Conversions API, Google Ads, Google Analytics 4, OpenAI Ads,
Convert, or your own webhook. Each destination has a **trigger**, the events
that make it fire.

Open a client and choose **Destinations**. Destinations are grouped by
platform, with the last 24 hours of deliveries for each.

## Read the list

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Destination** | The name, with a status dot (below). Click it to see its details |
| **Delivered** | Events the platform accepted in the last 24 hours |
| **Failed** | Deliveries the platform refused or that errored |
| **Skipped** | Events that matched the trigger but were not sent, with a reason on each delivery |
| **Last** | When the last delivery happened |

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Healthy** | Deliveries in the last 24 hours, none failed |
| **Some failures** | Some deliveries failed, but 90% or more succeeded |
| **Failing** | Fewer than 90% of deliveries succeeded |
| **Credential rejected** | The platform refused the stored login or token. Reconnect it |
| **No deliveries in 24h** | Nothing matched the trigger. Check the trigger and the client's traffic |
| **Paused** | The destination is turned off |

Skipped is often normal. A Convert destination skips visitors who weren't in a
running experiment. Google destinations skip events with nothing Google can
match, such as no click ID or client ID. A destination also skips events over
its delivery cap (10,000 per hour by default).

## Destination details

Click a destination to see:

- **Trigger**: the events that fire it. "Any event" means it has no event
  filter.
- **Triggered (all time)**, **Success rate 24h** and **Avg latency 24h**.
- **Last error**, when the latest delivery failed.
- **Recent deliveries**: the last 20, each with its event, attempt number and
  result.

A Meta Conversions API destination also has a **Match quality** tab with Meta's
live score for each event. See [Meta match quality](/agency-app/meta-quality).

A Convert destination shows **Convert verification** beside the details: how
many conversions Mythic sent in the window, and how many Convert counted.

## Send a test

**Send test** sends one test event through an ad platform destination with its
stored credential. Use it after connecting a platform to confirm the setup.
Google tests are validated without being recorded in the account. Meta tests
are real sends, so they appear in Events Manager. Webhooks have no test: they
fire on real events.

## Connect an ad platform

The **Integrations** panel lists each platform and whether it is connected.
Click **Connect** next to Google Ads or Google Analytics to sign in with Google
in a new tab. **Stored credentials** lists the logins and tokens saved for this
client. Mythic never shows the secret itself.

If Connect is unavailable, Google sign-in isn't set up for your agency yet.

## Add a webhook

Click **Add destination** and enter a name, the URL that should receive the
events, and the trigger event, such as `$pageview` or `purchase`. Mythic sends
each matching event to the URL as JSON.

Ad platform destinations need a connected integration first. Ask the Mythic
assistant in the chat panel to set one up, or use the API.

## For developers

Everything on this screen is available on the
[Destinations API](/destinations-api/overview), including trigger rules,
event mapping and per-platform settings.
