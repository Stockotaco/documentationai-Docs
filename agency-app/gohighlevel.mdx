## Overview

Connecting GoHighLevel installs the Mythic app on the client's GoHighLevel
sub-account. Mythic then receives the sub-account's CRM activity, such as new
contacts, form submissions, booked appointments, opportunity changes and
orders, and joins it to the visitors Mythic tracked on the client's website.
That join is what lets journeys, attribution and ROAS reach the lead or sale
recorded in GoHighLevel.

You need a GoHighLevel login with access to the client's sub-account.

## Connect a client

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Open the client and choose **Setup**, then **Integrations**. The
    **Connect GoHighLevel** step is at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the install">
    Click **Connect GoHighLevel**. The app opens GoHighLevel in the same tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick one sub-account">
    Log in to GoHighLevel and choose the client's **sub-account**. Do not pick
    the agency: an agency-level install is not linked to any client.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Come back to Mythic">
    GoHighLevel returns you to **Integrations**. A **GoHighLevel connected**
    banner confirms it, and the step shows the sub-account id and when it was
    connected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The first CRM events arrive as activity happens in the sub-account. See them on
the client's [Activity](/agency-app/activity) screen.

## Status

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Connected** | The sub-account is linked and Mythic receives its activity |
| **Not connected** | No sub-account is linked yet |
| **Disconnected** | GoHighLevel no longer accepts the install's access, for example after the app was uninstalled. Click **Reconnect** |
| **Incomplete** | GoHighLevel reports the install, but Mythic is missing the sub-account or its access key. Click **Reconnect** |

**Reconnect** runs the same install again. Pick the same sub-account.

## If it does not connect

The **GoHighLevel didn't connect** banner says why:

- **That sub-account is already linked to another client.** One GoHighLevel
  sub-account maps to exactly one Mythic client. Pick this client's own
  sub-account. To move a sub-account between clients, contact support.
- **No install was found.** The agency was picked instead of a sub-account, or
  the install was cancelled. Try again and pick a single sub-account.
- **Mythic reported a code.** Try again. If it repeats, send the code to
  support.

The button is unavailable on read-only sample clients.

## Where you come back to

Mythic returns you to this app's address after the install. That address must
be listed under **Callback origins** in [Settings](/agency-app/settings).
Connecting from the app adds it for you. If you open the app from a new
address, such as your own portal domain, check that it is listed.

## What the CRM data powers

- [Activity](/agency-app/activity): GoHighLevel events appear with the website
  events. GoHighLevel tag changes are hidden unless **Internal events** is on.
- [People & segments](/agency-app/people): journeys can use GoHighLevel
  appointment and opportunity outcomes as the goal, and GoHighLevel order or
  payment amounts as revenue.
