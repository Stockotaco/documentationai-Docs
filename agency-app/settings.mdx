## Overview

The **Settings** screen sets your agency profile, what your clients see when
they log in, and where connection flows such as GoHighLevel return. Choose
**Settings** in the agency navigation.

## Agency

- **Agency name**: the name clients see. Leave it blank to use your Mythic
  agency name.
- **Portal domain**: where clients log in, such as
  `insights.youragency.com`. The app stores it but does not set up DNS. Point
  the domain at the app yourself.
- **Let clients log in**: client users see only their own site, read-only.
- **Client chat**: lets client users ask the assistant about their own data.
  It is read-only and cannot change tracking or destinations.

Click **Save changes**.

## Portal members

Invite a client user to give them read-only access to their site:

1. Enter their **Email**.
2. Pick the **Client**.
3. Click **Invite**.

Each member shows a status: **Invited** (has not signed in yet), **Active**
or **Removed**, with the client and their last login. **Re-invite** sends the
invite again. **Remove** takes away their access immediately. You can invite
them again later.

Invited members cannot sign in until **Let clients log in** is on and saved.
Clients see only the dashboards you share with them. See
[Dashboards](/agency-app/dashboards).

## Callback origins

When you connect a service such as GoHighLevel, Mythic returns you to this
app afterwards. **Callback origins** lists the addresses it may return to,
one per line, such as `https://app.youragency.com`. Paths are ignored. Up to
20.

If the screen warns that this app's origin is not registered, click **Save
callback origins**. Connecting GoHighLevel from the app also registers it.
