## Overview

Open a client and choose **Scouts** from its menu. The screen has three tabs:

- **Inbox**: the reports your scouts filed for this client.
- **Signals**: every problem Mythic's checks found for this client, and
  whether a report covers it yet.
- **Scouts**: the scouts that run for this client. Click **New scout** to add
  one.

Scouts are for your agency. Client portal users don't see them.

## Scouts

A scout is an agent that runs on a schedule, reads one client's data (errors,
server logs, events, sessions, short links, destinations) and files one
report per problem it finds. Each scout follows a skill:

| Skill | What it looks for |
| --- | --- |
| Error tracking | Browser errors and server error logs that cost visitors, leads or tracking |
| Attribution drift | Channels losing credit, such as paid traffic arriving without UTMs or click ids |
| Conversion delivery | Conversions that never reach the ad platforms, and lost credentials |
| Signal triage | Groups the client's open signals into one report per problem. See [How triage groups signals](#how-triage-groups-signals) |
| Custom | Your own instructions over every surface a scout can read |

Scouts run every day, on weekdays or weekly at a time you pick in the
client's time zone. Signal triage runs hourly. A client can have up to 10
scouts.

Scouts run on your own OpenRouter, Gemini or Cloudflare model key, so the
provider bills you directly. Add a key under **Replay Vision**, **Model keys**,
for the client or the agency. Without a key, Mythic won't create or run a
scout. Each scout's page shows its runs, what each cost and what it read.

## The Inbox

Each report is one problem: a title, a summary, a suggested action, a
priority from P0 (conversions or tracking broken now) to P4 (informational)
and the evidence behind it. Evidence is the exact tool results the scout read,
plus any signals it grouped.

When a scout sees the same problem again, it updates the open report with
fresh evidence instead of filing another one.

The Inbox shows **Open** and **Snoozed** reports by default. Filter by
status, priority or scout.

| Status | Meaning | What you can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open | Needs attention | Resolve, snooze or dismiss |
| Snoozed | Hidden until a date, then it reopens on its own | Unsnooze, resolve or dismiss |
| Resolved | Fixed. If it comes back, the scout files a new report | Nothing |
| Dismissed | Not worth acting on | Reopen |
| Suppressed | The scout filed it for the record, with no action needed | Restore or dismiss |

- **Dismiss** needs a reason. The scout reads it on its next runs for 30
  days, so it stops filing the same thing.
- **Snooze** lasts up to 90 days.

To get new reports in Slack or a webhook, pick channels in the scout's
**Delivery** step. Each new open report is sent once. Updates and suppressed
reports are not sent.

## Signals

A signal is one problem a Mythic check found on its own: a new browser error,
a destination rejecting conversions, a short link that's down. Every problem
that meets a check's threshold becomes a signal. Nothing is judged or
filtered, and no model is involved.

Signals, alerts and reports are related:

- An **alert** is what a check raises. The [Alerts](/agency-app/alerts) screen
  shows all of them, including agency-level ones such as usage, and rules
  decide which go to Slack or a webhook.
- A **signal** is an alert about one client, shown on that client's
  **Signals** tab. It is the input for Signal triage.
- A **report** is what a scout writes after looking into a problem. One
  report can group several related signals.

A signal resolves on its own when its check sees the problem recover.
Replay Vision signals are the exception: they stay open until someone
resolves them on the Alerts screen.

### What raises a signal

| Source | Raised when | Severity | How often |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Browser errors | A new error reaches 3 or more sessions in 24 hours | Warning, critical at 100+ sessions | Daily, about 08:40 UTC |
| Browser errors | An error spikes to 10 or more sessions in 24 hours and 3 times its 7-day average | Warning, critical at 100+ sessions | Daily, about 08:40 UTC |
| Destination delivery | 5 deliveries fail in a row, or under 50% succeed over 10 or more attempts in 24 hours | Warning | Hourly |
| Destination delivery | The platform rejects the credential (HTTP 401 or 403) | Critical | Hourly |
| Event contracts | Events break their contract | Warning | Daily, about 08:00 UTC |
| Event contracts | The client sent no events all day | Critical | Daily, about 08:00 UTC |
| Link uptime | A short link fails 2 checks in a row | Critical | Every 5 minutes |
| Data syncs | A sync Mythic manages fails | Warning | On each sync |
| Session health | The site keeps starting new sessions for the same visitor | Warning | Daily |
| Experiments | An incrementality experiment has new outlier days | Info | Daily |
| Replay Vision | An opted-in monitor answers yes, unless the second check disagrees | Warning | After a scan |

Browser error signals need history to compare against. A client needs 97
days of error history before new-error signals start, and 8 days before spike
signals start.

## How triage groups signals

Add a scout with the **Signal triage** skill to group a client's signals.
On the **Signals** tab, **Add Signal triage scout** opens the form with the
skill already picked.

Every hour, Mythic checks whether the client has a signal no report covers
yet, or one that changed severity. If not, nothing runs and nothing is spent.
If so, the scout reads the new signals, checks the client's data where it
matters, and either adds each signal to the open report that covers its
problem or files a new report. Several related signals become one report, so
you handle one problem instead of many alerts.

Triage runs on your model key, like every scout, and you pay only for the
hours it actually runs. A new signal can take up to an hour to appear in a
report.

Without a triage scout, or without a model key, signals stay ungrouped on the
**Signals** tab. They still appear on the Alerts screen.

## Use signals day to day

Work from the **Inbox**. With Signal triage on, the signals behind each
report are listed on the report under **Signals behind this report**.

Check the **Signals** tab for what isn't in a report yet:

- The strip at the top shows whether triage is on, and how many signals are
  not grouped, in a report, or resolved in the last 7 days.
- Switch between **Not grouped**, **In a report** and **Resolved**, and filter
  by source.
- Click a signal to open its details: what the check saw, the report it is
  in, what the problem usually means and what usually fixes it, and a button
  to the related screen, such as Errors or Destinations. Turn on **Raw** to
  see the check's data as JSON.

You don't need to close signals. Fix the problem and the signal resolves
when its check next runs.

## Privacy

Browser error, destination, session health and Replay Vision signals can
contain visitor data. On HIPAA clients these signals show a lock and
**Restricted (HIPAA)** instead of their text and details. If Mythic can't
confirm a client's privacy setting, they show **Restricted for now**. Try
again in a minute.
