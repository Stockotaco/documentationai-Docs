## Overview

A **session** is one visit to the client's site. The **Session replay** screen
lists them newest first. Open a client and choose **Sessions**.

On a wide screen the page has three columns: the recordings list, the replay,
and an inspector. On a narrow screen (or with the chat panel open) they stack,
and the inspector sits under **Session details**.

## The recordings list

Each row shows the visitor (their user id, or "Anonymous" plus the end of the
visitor id), how long ago the visit started, and a dot for engagement:

- **Engaged**: more than one page view, at least 10 seconds long, or an event
  other than a page view.
- **Bounced**: none of those.

The second line shows the visitor's country, device, page views, event count,
a play icon if the visit was recorded, and the duration. The third line is the
landing page. Use **All / Engaged / Bounced** to filter and the refresh button
to reload. The list is sorted newest first.

## The replay

The bar above the player shows the landing page with three links:

- **Person** opens the visitor's [profile](/agency-app/people). Anonymous
  visitors have a profile too.
- **Open page** opens the landing page in a new tab.
- **Copy link** copies a link to this session.

Click **Play replay** to watch the visit. If the visitor had more than one
browser tab open, choose the tab to watch. **Summarize this session** asks the
assistant in the chat panel to describe what happened.

## The inspector

- **Overview**: session id, visitor (links to the person), landing page (opens
  the page), a link to the landing page's [heatmap](/agency-app/heatmaps),
  start time, duration, pages, events, location, device, source and how long
  the replay is kept.
- **Events**: every page view, page exit, form interaction, identify and
  custom event in order, with the time from the start of the session. Type in
  **Search events** to filter. Click an event to jump the replay to that
  moment once it has loaded. Event times are approximate (whole seconds).
- **Observations**: [Replay Vision](/agency-app/replay-vision) results for the
  recording, if a scanner has watched it. You can scan the session from here.
  Shown for recorded sessions only.

## What gets recorded

Recording is turned on per client with **Record session replays** in
[Tracking setup](/agency-app/tracking), which also sets the share of visits
recorded. A visit without a play icon was not recorded: it fell outside that
share, lasted under 10 seconds, or happened before recording was on.
Recordings are kept for 30 days. Typed values are masked in the replay unless
masking was turned off for the client. HIPAA clients have no recordings.
