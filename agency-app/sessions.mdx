## Overview

A **session** is one visit to the client's site. The **Sessions** screen lists
them newest first, with a replay and a timeline for each. Open a client and
choose **Sessions**.

## The list

Each session shows the visitor, when the visit started, how long it lasted,
and the landing page. The dot shows whether the visit was engaged:

- **Engaged**: more than one page view, at least 10 seconds long, or an event
  other than a page view.
- **Bounced**: none of those.

A play icon means the visit was recorded. Use **Engagement** to show only
engaged or only bounced visits.

## A session

Click a session to see:

- **Session replay**: click **Play replay** to watch the visit. If the
  visitor had more than one browser tab open, choose the tab to watch.
- **Timeline**: each page view, click, form interaction, error and custom
  event in order. The timeline is built from the recording.
- **Details**: device, pages viewed, duration and traffic source.
- **Replay Vision** results for the recording, if a scanner has watched it.
  You can scan the session from here. See [Replay Vision](/agency-app/replay-vision).

**Copy session link** copies a link to this session. **Summarize this
session** asks the assistant in the chat panel to describe what happened.

## What gets recorded

Recording is turned on per client with **Record session replays** in
[Tracking setup](/agency-app/tracking), which also sets the share of visits
recorded. A visit without a play icon was not recorded: it fell outside that
share, lasted under 10 seconds, or happened before recording was on.
Recordings are kept for 30 days. Typed values are masked in the replay unless
masking was turned off for the client. HIPAA clients have no recordings.

## For developers
