## Overview

Mythic watches your clients for you: event contracts, short link uptime, data
syncs, incrementality experiments and usage. When one of these checks finds a
problem, it opens an **alert**. The **Alerts** screen has three tabs:

- **Feed**: the alerts.
- **Notifications**: set up and manage Slack and webhook destinations.
- **Rules**: advanced controls for which alerts go where.

Open **Alerts** from the agency menu to see every client, or from a client's
menu to see only that client.

## What raises an alert

| Check | Raised when | Severity | How often |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Event contracts | Events break their contract | Warning | Daily, about 08:00 UTC |
| Event contracts | The client sent no events all day | Critical | Daily, about 08:00 UTC |
| Link uptime | A short link fails 2 checks in a row | Critical | Every 5 minutes |
| Data syncs | A sync Mythic manages fails | Warning | On each sync |
| Experiments | An incrementality experiment has new outlier days | Info | Daily |
| Usage | Your agency passes 80% of a monthly event quota | Info | Daily |
| Usage | Your agency reaches 100% of a monthly event quota | Warning | Daily |
| Replay Vision | An opted-in monitor answers yes, unless the second check disagrees | Warning | After a scan |

Each check decides what counts as broken. Rules only decide who hears about
it.

## The feed

The feed shows **Open** alerts by default. Switch to **Resolved** or **All**
to see older ones. Each alert shows its client, the check, when it opened, and
how many times the check has seen it since.

One problem is one alert. It notifies your channels when it opens, and again
if it changes, such as a different set of failing contracts or a higher
severity. When the check stops finding the problem, the alert resolves on its
own. If it comes back later, a new alert opens. Replay Vision findings stay
open until you resolve them. A scanner shares one open alert, which updates
when another session is flagged.

- **Open** goes to the screen of the check that raised it, such as the
  Replay Vision observation, Link uptime or your agency's usage.
- **Resolve** closes an alert by hand. It sends no notification.
- **Investigate** starts a new chat thread with the alert, so the assistant
  can look into the cause with the client's data without mixing in an earlier
  conversation.

## Send alerts to Slack or a webhook

Click **Set up notifications**. One guided flow connects the destination,
tests delivery and creates its notification rule.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a destination">
    Choose **Slack** or **Webhook**, paste its HTTPS URL and optionally name it.
    Slack needs an Incoming Webhook URL (`https://hooks.slack.com/services/...`).
    Open **Get a Slack webhook** for setup instructions.

    Mythic stores the URL securely and shows only a masked version afterward.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test your connection">
    For a webhook, copy the signing secret and confirm you saved it. It is
    shown only once. Verify incoming deliveries using the
    `X-Mythic-Signature` header.

    Click **Send test**. Slack receives one test message. A webhook receives a
    signed test request and must return a 2xx response. Continue after the
    test succeeds. If it fails, retry or choose **Use a different URL**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your alerts">
    Choose **Warnings and critical alerts**, **Replay Vision findings** or
    **Choose specific checks** for custom sources and severity.

    On a client's screen, that client is selected by default. No client
    selection means all clients, including new ones. Click **Turn on
    notifications** to finish.

    Replay Vision sends only findings from monitor scanners with **Raise an
    alert** turned on. Classifications, scores and summaries do not notify.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Finish later** keeps the destination without adding a rule. Open
**Notifications**, choose **Manage**, then **Choose alerts** to finish.

## Manage notifications

Each destination shows whether it is on, not sending, paused or failing.
**Manage** lets you send another test, edit its rules, resume paused delivery
or remove the destination. A destination that fails 20 times in a row is
paused automatically.

For advanced routing, open **Rules** and click **New rule**. Choose checks,
clients, minimum severity and one or more destinations. Empty checks means
every source. **Also notify when resolved** sends a message when a problem
clears. Replay Vision's guided preset leaves this off.

Rules can be turned off with their switch or deleted. Deleting a rule or a
destination keeps its alerts in the feed.

Email isn't available as a channel yet.
