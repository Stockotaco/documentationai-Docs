## Agency overview

The agency **Overview** shows tracking health and results across every
client for the last 7 full days. Choose **Overview** in the agency navigation.

| Tile | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Active clients** | Clients that are not paused, out of all clients. |
| **Events** | Events received across all clients. |
| **Sessions** | Visits across all clients. |
| **Failed deliveries (24h)** | Deliveries to destinations that failed in the last 24 hours. |

**Events by client** charts each client's events. **Client health** counts
clients as **Healthy**, **Needs attention** or **Broken**.

**Needs attention** lists each client with a problem:

| Problem | Level |
| --- | --- |
| Client is disabled | Warning |
| Couldn't check the last event | Warning |
| No events in the last 24 hours | Error |
| Failed deliveries to a destination | Error |

Click **Ask agent** on a client to work through the problem in the chat
panel, or **All alerts** to open [Alerts](/agency-app/alerts). **Ask for a
roster summary** asks the assistant to summarize every client's week. When
nothing needs attention, every client is enabled, sent events in the last 24
hours and has no failed deliveries.

## Client overview

Open a client to see its week at a glance, compared with the previous 7
days:

- **Sessions**: visits.
- **Conversions**: the count of the chosen **Conversion event**.
- **Revenue** for stores, or **Leads** for other clients.
- **Visitors identified**: the share of visits from people Mythic knows by
  email or user id.

**Sessions** charts the last 30 days. **Conversion funnel** shows how many
people went from a visit to the conversion in the last 7 days. Add steps
between them to see where people drop off. A client that only sends page
views has no funnel. Track a form submission, booking or purchase to see one.

Client users who log in to the portal see this screen as their home page.
