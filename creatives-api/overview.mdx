## Overview

Mythic reads each client's own Meta ads from the Airbyte data it already syncs: ads, creatives, videos, images and ad insights. It makes no live calls to the Meta API.

Every ad that is active, or had any spend in the last 90 days, is covered. There is no spend minimum, so new and zero-spend ads are included. For every covered ad Mythic:

1. Stores the copy, headline, call to action, landing link, format (image, video, carousel or dynamic), carousel cards and dynamic creative variants, campaign, ad set and ad names, and 90-day spend and impressions.
2. Copies the best available image and the video to Mythic storage, per client (never shared between clients). Meta's media links expire within days, so every sync looks for a better live version: the full image first, then the creative image, then the video still, and the small thumbnail only as a last resort (marked `quality: "thumbnail"`). A stored copy is never replaced by a worse or expired one.
3. Transcribes the video with sentence timestamps.
4. Reads the creative and its transcript with the same models and tags as [Competitor Ads](/competitor-ads-api/overview): hook, offer and call to action text, hook type, persuasion angle and offer tags, and the E.P.I.C. test. For a carousel the models also read the cards; for a dynamic creative, its other headlines, bodies and descriptions. These extra versions are capped at 6 entries, 600 characters each and 3,000 characters in total, so cards or variants past the cap are not read. The E.P.I.C. test reads the primary copy.

An unchanged creative is never analysed again.

The summary has the same shape as the competitor ads summary, so what a client runs compares directly with what its competitors run.

## When data updates

- **Mythic-managed Airbyte connection:** within 5 minutes of each successful Meta sync.
- **Legacy dataset:** once a day after 09:00 UTC.
- **By hand:** `POST /creatives/sync`, at most once every 10 minutes and 10 times per 24 hours per client.

A client with many ads is read over several passes, about 3,000 ads per pass, 5 minutes apart. `POST /creatives/sync` runs one pass and returns `complete: false` when more follow; `GET /creatives/sync` shows a read in progress under `traversal`.

An ad that is no longer active and had no spend in 90 days is marked `gone` when a complete read no longer finds it. A read that has not finished never marks ads as gone.

## Read creatives

```bash
curl "https://mythic-analytics.gulp.workers.dev/client/v1/creatives?location_id=LOC&status=ACTIVE&format=video" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer ak_..."
```

Each creative carries `landing_page` (`{ url, domain, path }`, the normalized link, as competitor ads), `congruency` (see below), `spend_90d` (from the sync) and `spend_30d` (from Mythic's ad spend copy; `null` with a `spend_error` when it could not be read). Filters: `account_id`, `campaign_id`, `adset_id`, `status` (a Meta `effective_status`, or `gone`), `format`, `has_video`, `tag`, `epic=in_order`, `since`, `include_gone`. Page with `cursor`.

`GET /creatives/{ad_id}` returns one ad by its Meta ad id, with the full analysis, the transcript and media URLs.

<Callout kind="info">
Media URLs are signed and valid for about an hour. Read the creative again for fresh links.
</Callout>

## Landing page congruency

Does the landing page deliver what the ad promises? Mythic fetches the page every ad points at and scores each ad against it.

- **Pages.** One per client and normalized landing URL, shared by every ad pointing at it. The page is rendered in a browser, its text is kept (bounded to 12,000 characters, plus its title, main headline and above-the-fold text) and tagged with the same hook type, angle and offer tags as ads. Pages are fetched when a first ad points at them, again every 7 days, and when a new ad points at a page fetched more than a day ago.
- **Not fetched.** Links that are not a web page get `status: "skipped"` and a `skip_reason`: `lead_form` (Meta lead forms such as `fb.me`), `messenger` (Messenger, WhatsApp), `instant_experience`, `app_store`, `social_profile` (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and similar profiles) or `redirector` (link shorteners). A link that redirects to one of these is skipped too.
- **Score.** For each ad copy and page version, one model call compares the ad's hook, offer, CTA and claims with the page's headline, offer and CTA. `score` is 0 to 100 (100: the page delivers everything the ad promises), `band` is `high` (70+), `medium` (40 to 69) or `low` (under 40). Ads with the same copy pointing at the same page share one score. A changed page is re-scored; the last score stays readable with `current: false` until then.
- **Mismatches.** Up to 8, each with `kind` (`offer`, `price`, `claim`, `headline`, `cta`, `product`, `audience`, `other`), `severity`, `ad_quote` and `page_quote` (null when the page lacks it, `missing_on_page: true`). Every quote is checked against the ad and page text, and a mismatch whose quote is not there is dropped (`dropped_mismatches`).
- **Tag diff.** `tag_diff` is computed without a model: offer elements the ad has and the page lacks (`offer.missing_on_page`), shared angles and their overlap, whether the page opens with the ad's hook type, and `headline_coverage`, the share of the ad hook's words found in the page headline and above-the-fold text.

Every ad in the list and detail carries `congruency`:

| `status` | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `scored` | `score`, `band`, `summary` and `mismatch_count` are set. The detail adds `mismatches`, `tag_diff` and the model cost. |
| `pending` | Waiting for the page fetch or the score. A previous score may be shown with `current: false`. |
| `failed` | Scoring failed 3 times for this page version. |
| `page_unavailable` | The page is `skipped` (see `skip_reason`) or could not be fetched. |
| `not_queued` | The ad's analysis is not done yet. |

`congruency` is `null` when the ad has no http(s) link.

`GET /creatives/landing-pages` lists the pages this client's own ads pointed at in the last 2 days (`all=true` for every page), with `status`, title, headline and `congruency` stats: `pairs`, `scored`, `avg_score`, `min_score` and counts per band. Filters: `status`, `domain`. `GET /creatives/landing-pages/{id}` adds the page text, its analysis and up to 50 ads pointing at it with their congruency and mismatches.

The detail also carries `post_click`: sessions from the Mythic SDK on the client's site whose entry page is this page (host and path, query ignored) in the last 30 days, with `meta_sessions` (an `fbclid` or a Facebook/Instagram `utm_source`), `bounce_rate` (one pageview or fewer), `avg_pageviews` and `avg_duration_s`. Conversions are not included. `post_click` is `null` with a `post_click_error` when it could not be read.

<Callout kind="note">
Page text is copied from a public web page and treated as untrusted data in the model prompt. The score describes message match, not page quality or conversion rate.
</Callout>

## Compare with competitors

```bash
curl "https://mythic-analytics.gulp.workers.dev/client/v1/creatives/summary?location_id=LOC" -H "Authorization: Bearer ak_..."
curl "https://mythic-analytics.gulp.workers.dev/client/v1/competitor-ads/summary?location_id=LOC" -H "Authorization: Bearer ak_..."
```

Both return `overall` with the same fields: ad and active counts, formats, new ads in the last 7 and 30 days, hook type, angle and offer shares among analysed ads, the longest-running active ads, the most used hooks and offers, and E.P.I.C. aggregates. The creatives summary groups by ad account (`accounts`) where the competitor summary groups by page.

<Callout kind="note">
The E.P.I.C. test is Mythic's interpretation of Chris Rocheleau's publicly described formula. It is not yet checked against spend results.
</Callout>

## Complete readiness and coverage

`GET /client/v1/creatives/intelligence` (`get_creative_intelligence` in MCP) needs
`creative_analysis:read` and the `creative_analysis` plan feature, with the same
location binding and privacy rules as other creatives reads. No model call or
live Meta request is made.

The response contains exact counts over **every stored non-gone ad**, not a
sample page: active/spending ads, unknown spend, missing creatives, analysis
coverage, and media/transcript/analysis states with ratios. Transcript counts are
eligible ads, not unique shared videos. Source Airbyte errors/freshness and
Mythic traversal freshness are separate. No raw error text or credentials appear.
An absent source or timestamp is unknown, not healthy. A source is confirmed
healthy only when every connection is active and has synced. Partial traversals
are open findings; in-progress, skipped or unknown syncs cannot confirm recovery.
Normal short queues are not alarms, and retrying or incomplete stages do not
confirm recovery. Analysis/transcript work older than 48 hours merits investigation;
media enqueue age is not available, so pending media is not labeled stuck.

`cohorts` lists up to 10 account/campaign groups by ad count, reduced further to
keep the complete JSON response under 10,000 characters. `cohorts_total` and
`cohorts_truncated` disclose omissions. Each group has hook-type, angle, offer
and format counts, known raw spend, within-cohort shares and example `ad_id`s.
A rich campaign may retain only its highest-ad-count dimension rows, preserving
at least one row per family and every unclassified gap. `dimensions_total` and
`dimensions_truncated` disclose omissions; shares still use the whole campaign,
not the retained rows. Ratios are rounded to six decimal places.
Multi-label tag shares overlap; unclassified values are coverage gaps. A partial
sync can mean incomplete upstream coverage even when stored-row counts are exact.

`performance.status` is currently `unavailable`: these snapshots have no verified
ad-level outcome, campaign-objective, currency and attribution-window join.
Do not calculate CPA/ROAS, claim success from spend, pool accounts/campaigns or
mistake creation age for continuous runtime. Spend is a rolling 90-day source
snapshot, not a daily or weekly performance series.

The opt-in **Creative performance** weekly scout includes coverage and next-test
hypotheses in one ordinary report. The daily **Creative readiness** scout monitors
queues and source readiness. Both are agency BYOK only and use existing Scout
schedules, settings and delivery channels. See [Scouts](/agency-api/scouts#own-ad-creative-scouts).
